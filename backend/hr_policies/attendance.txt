ATTENDANCE POLICY

Standard Work Hours:
- General Shift: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Monday to Friday).
- Flexible timing allowed: Check-in between 8:30 AM - 10:30 AM. Total 9 hours mandatory.

Punch-in/Punch-out:
- All employees must mark attendance via biometric or the HR mobile app app.
- Failing to punch in/out will result in "Half Day" marking.

Remote Work:
- Work from Home (WFH) is allowed up to 4 days a month with manager approval.
- Remote employees must be reachable on Slack/Teams during work hours.

Late Arrival:
- 3 late arrivals (> 15 mins) in a month = 1/2 day CL deduction.
