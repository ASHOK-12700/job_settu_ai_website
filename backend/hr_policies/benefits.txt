BENEFITS & PROBATION

Probation Period:
- Standard probation is 3 months from Date of Joining.
- Confirmation depends on performance review by the manager.
- During probation, notice period is 15 days; after confirmation, it is 60 days.

Health Insurance:
- Group Medical Cover of INR 5 Lakhs for employee + spouse + 2 children.
- Parents can be added with an additional premium deduction (optional).
- Coverage starts from Day 1 of employment.

Wellness:
- Free annual health checkup at partner hospitals.
- Gym membership reimbursement up to INR 15,000 per annum.
