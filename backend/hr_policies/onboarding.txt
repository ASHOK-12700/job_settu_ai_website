ONBOARDING STEPS

1. **Offer Acceptance & Documentation**: Sign the offer letter digitally. Upload required documents (ID proof, Educational Certificates, Previous Relieving Letter) to the HR Portal within 48 hours.
2. **IT & Logistics**:
   - Confirm your shipping address for the laptop kit.
   - You will receive a tracking ID 2 days before joining.
   - Login credentials for Email/Slack will be sent to your personal email 24 hours prior to joining.
3. **Day 1 (Virtual/Office)**:
   - Join the "New Hire Orientation" call at 10:00 AM IST.
   - Meet your Buddy and Manager.
   - Complete the "InfoSec Awareness" module on the Learning Portal.
4. **First Week**:
   - Complete mandatory compliance training.
   - Set up your benefits (Health Insurance nominees).
   - Join team standups and introduction meetings.
