LEAVE POLICY

Types of Leave:
1. Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per year. Prorated from joining date. catch-all for personal matters.
2. Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per year. Medical certificate required for > 2 consecutive days.
3. Earned Leave (EL): 15 days per year modeled on days worked. Eligible after probation.
4. Maternity/Paternity Leave: As per government statutory regulations.

Rules:
- Leaves must be applied for on the HR Portal at least 2 days in advance (except Sick Leave).
- Managers must approve leaves within 24 hours.
- Unused PL (Privilege/Earned Leave) can be carried forward up to 45 days.
- CL and SL cannot be encased.
- Unauthorized absence for > 3 days will be treated as loss of pay and potential disciplinary action.
